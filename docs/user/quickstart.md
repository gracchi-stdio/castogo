# Quickstart

A short walkthrough for publishing your first episode.

## 1. Sign in

Go to `/login` and sign in with your admin account.

## 2. Configure your podcast

Open **Settings** from the sidebar and set your podcast title, cover image, and
storage credentials.

## 3. Create an episode

1. Open **New Episode** from the sidebar.
2. Enter a title and upload your audio file.
3. Click **Publish** when you're ready.

That's it — your RSS feed at `/feed` now includes the new episode.
